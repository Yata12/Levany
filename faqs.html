FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Contact Us

1. What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

2. Do you offer pickup and delivery services?

Yes. We offer convenient pickup and delivery services. Simply schedule a time through our website or call us directly.

3. How long does it take to get my laundry done?

Standard time is 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day services are available at an additional cost.

4. Are my clothes safe with you?

Absolutely. We use top-quality detergents and equipment, and our trained staff ensures your clothes are treated with the utmost care.

5. How do I schedule a service?

You can book your service online through our website, via WhatsApp, or by calling our customer service line.

6. What if I am not satisfied with the service?

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with our service, please let us know within 24 hours and we will make it right.

7. Do you offer laundry services for businesses?

Yes. We provide bulk and contract laundry services for hotels, salons, gyms, and more. Contact us for customized business packages.
