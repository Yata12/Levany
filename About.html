About Us

Levany Fresh Laundry was born out of a simple idea — to bring freshness, reliability, and trust into every home in our beloved city. From humble beginnings, we’ve grown into one of Kampala’s most trusted laundry services, proudly serving families, students, professionals, and businesses.

We believe clean clothes are more than just hygiene — they are confidence, dignity, and care. That is why every shirt, dress, and bedsheet that passes through our hands is treated with love and attention to detail.

Our Mission

To make laundry easy, affordable, and accessible for every household in Kampala.

Our Vision

To be the city’s most trusted and loved laundry partner — fresh, fast, and friendly.

Our Values

Care. Honesty. Quality. Community. We treat every client like family.
