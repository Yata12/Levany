About Levany

Levany Fresh Laundry was born out of a simple idea to bring freshness, reliability, and trust into every home in our beloved city. From humble beginnings, we’ve grown into one of Kampala’s most trusted laundry services, proudly serving families, students, professionals, and businesses.

About Our Services

We provide a wide range of professional laundry services designed to meet the daily needs of individuals, families, and businesses. From regular washing and drying to expert ironing, folding, and dry cleaning, each garment is treated with care using high-quality detergents and modern machines to ensure freshness, cleanliness, and quality results.

In addition to personal laundry, customers enjoy convenient pick-up and delivery options that save time and effort. Businesses such as hotels, salons, gyms, and offices can also benefit from tailored bulk and corporate laundry solutions. .
